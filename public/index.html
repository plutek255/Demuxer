Overview

What is the Demuxer?

The Plutek Demuxer is a wearable cybernetic glove designed for gaming and PC control. It maps hand gestures and finger flex inputs to USB HID output — making your hand the controller.

🖐️

Flex Sensor Input

5 flex sensors across the fingers map bend angles to keyboard/mouse or custom actions via the .dmxr scripting system.

📟

1.3" OLED Display

Wrist-mounted OLED shows active mode, connection status, and gesture feedback in real time.

📳

Haptic Feedback

18 mini vibration motors (coin type) driven by a DRV2605L haptic driver give tactile response to in-game events.

⚡

ESP32 Core

Powered by an ESP32 dev board with a 3.7V LiPo battery and TP4056 charging module. Wireless-capable out of the box.

💡

RGB Lighting

WS2812B LED strip runs along the glove. Fully programmable — sync to game state, mode, or any custom trigger.

🧠

.dmxr Scripting

Custom scripting language for defining gestures, modes, and output mappings. Supports TSX blocks and pixel art sprites.
